## 🔬 Identification & Laser Marking Note
Due to the microscopic laser marking font utilized by SK hynix on physical BGA packages, the official number **"9"** in the prefix **"H9HP"** has a straight lower leg that is frequently misread or scanned via OCR as the letter **"Q"** (**"HQHP"**). Both **H9HP52AECMMD** and **HQHP52AECMMD** refer to the identical physical memory component.
